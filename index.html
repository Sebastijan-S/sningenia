FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped individually. Send a short brief and we'll reply within 1 hour on business days with a rough range and timeline.

How long does a project take?

A focused small-business site usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Larger multi-page sites take 6 to 10 weeks, depending on content and how quickly feedback comes in.

Do you work with big companies or only small businesses?

Both. We work with local business owners, startups and established companies. The process is the same: clear goals, clear scope, direct communication.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your business and goals, examples you like, and any logo or content you already have. We help with the structure and wording.

Can I edit the website myself after launch?

Yes. We build on Webflow or Framer so you can update text and images yourself, and we walk you through it at handover.

Do you build sites in Serbian and English?

Yes. We can build in Serbian, English, or both.

What does "AI integration" mean?

Practical features that save you time, such as chat assistants that answer common questions, automatic lead replies, or content workflows. We only suggest it when it helps.

Who will I be working with?

Directly with our two co-founders: a creative lead and a developer. No account managers in between.
