Privacy Policy

Who is responsible for your data

Sebastijan Sekosan and Nikola Kindlajn are the data controllers for personal data handled through this website. They are freelancers operating under the name SNIngenia and are based in Serbia.

What data we collect

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share so we can reply to your enquiry and arrange an introductory call. This website has no contact form or direct booking system.

Why we use your data and our legal basis

We use your information to reply to your enquiry and prepare a project offer. Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing without your consent.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry information for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless a contract or legal requirement means we need to keep it longer.

Who receives your data

Messages sent to contact@sningenia.com are received by our team through our email service provider. We do not sell or share enquiry information for advertising.

Analytics

This site currently loads no Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity and sets no analytics cookies. We manually track received enquiries and mutually agreed calls. An email-link click is not a received enquiry or a confirmed call.

Your rights

Under Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection and, where it applies, the GDPR, you may have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, request portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia.

How to contact us

For privacy requests, email sebastian@sningenia.com or nikola@sningenia.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The date below shows when it was last updated.
