FAQ

Questions, answered

How do we arrange the introductory call?

Email a short description of your business, website needs and a few suitable times with your timezone. We agree a 10-minute call by reply. The call is confirmed only when both sides agree on a time.

How do you set the price and timeline?

We prepare a proposal and schedule after agreeing the scope, content and features. We discuss pricing with you privately.

What do you need from us?

Business information, the purpose of the website, your logo and available text and images. We agree who prepares content and who approves each stage.

What about revisions and extra features?

The proposal defines the included revisions, pages and features, and how additional work is agreed before work starts.

Who pays for domains, hosting and maintenance?

Domains, hosting, subscriptions and maintenance are listed separately in the proposal, with clear responsibilities and costs.

Can I update the website myself?

We choose the platform around your editing needs. Access, training and support are agreed before the build.

Do you work across industries and in both languages?

Yes. We work with businesses across industries. Your website can be in English, Serbian or both.
